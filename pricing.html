Pricing

Our prices are on this page because you should not have to ask. The figures below are starting points for standard properties. Location and condition change them, and we confirm the exact figure before anything is scheduled.

First visit

₹2,500 within Bengaluru city limits · ₹3,500 for outskirts

One visit. Full report. Fixed price confirmed before we go.

Take a plan within 30 days and this fee is credited in full against your first quarter.

We charge for the first visit rather than offering it free. A visit costs us a person's time, travel and half a day of report work. A service that gives that away has to recover it somewhere else, and you would end up paying for it without being told. We would rather charge ₹2,500 and do the job properly.

What changes the price

  • Size of the property
  • Distance. Travel time is the biggest variable. A site inside the city and a site beyond the ring road are not the same job.
  • Compound wall or structure
  • Visit frequency
  • Existing condition. If there is standing dumping, heavy overgrowth or a boundary already in question, that is one-time work quoted separately before regular visits begin.

Work beyond inspection

Clearing, weed removal, fencing, gate and signage work, and repairs are quoted per job. You approve the quote in writing before anything starts. You receive the vendor invoice and photographs of the completed work, and our charge is always shown separately from the vendor's.

Revision

Plan prices are held for the term of your agreement and reviewed annually. Statutory wage rates in Karnataka were revised in May 2026 and are reviewed by the state each year; where that materially changes our costs, we will tell you in advance rather than after.
