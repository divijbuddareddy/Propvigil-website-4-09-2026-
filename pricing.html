What's included

A clear breakdown of everything covered in your annual plan versus work quoted separately.

In your annual plan

  • Scheduled visits by a named person
  • A full photo report every visit
  • Short video walk-through
  • Boundary and encroachment check
  • Cleaning — waste, debris, overgrowth
  • Municipal notices tracked and flagged

Quoted separately

  • The first clearing of a neglected site
  • Fencing, gates, compound wall repair
  • Boundary marker replacement
  • Ownership signage
  • Resolving a municipal notice
Every quote is a single figure. Approved by you in writing. Photographed when it is done.

What changes your price

Size · Distance from the city · Walled or open site · Quarterly or monthly · What condition it is in now

Why there is no rate card here

A figure that changes once we have actually seen your property is worse than no figure at all. Send the location and you will have your price in writing, before anything is scheduled.
