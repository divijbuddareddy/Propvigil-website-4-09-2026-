What do you own?

Different properties fail in different ways. Tell us which one is yours.

Vacant land or site

▼
WHAT GOES WRONG WHEN NOBODY VISITS

Waste and debris get dumped. Boundary stones go missing. A neighbour's compound wall moves. Someone parks, stores material, or builds a shed. A municipal notice is issued and nobody tells you. Years pass and the site is no longer quite the shape it was.

WHAT WE CHECK ON EVERY VISIT
  • Boundary markers and stones — present, moved, missing
  • Encroachment: structures, storage, parking, sheds, crops
  • Waste, debris and construction dumping
  • Fence, gate and compound wall condition
  • Any notice, marking or posting on or near the site
  • Neighbouring construction affecting your boundary

Plus a further twelve checks on every visit, listed in full in your report.

WHAT YOU GET

A dated report with geotagged photographs, including the same fixed camera positions every visit so you can compare one quarter against the next. A short written observation. A flag if anything needs action.

AND THEN WE DO THE WORK

The report tells you what needs doing. We are the ones who do it. Waste and debris clearing. Weed and overgrowth removal. Fencing, gate and compound wall repair. Boundary marker replacement. Ownership signage. Coordination with the ward office where a notice has been issued.

Each job is quoted separately before anything starts, and nothing happens without your written approval. You get the vendor invoice, the disposal receipt where one is issued, and before-and-after photographs.

Locked or unoccupied flat

▼
WHAT GOES WRONG WHEN NOBODY VISITS

An empty flat deteriorates faster than empty land. Damp spreads behind furniture. A slow leak from the flat above goes unnoticed for a year. Seals perish, taps seize, drains dry out and let smells in. Pests settle. Society dues accumulate quietly until there is a notice on the door.

WHAT WE CHECK ON EVERY VISIT
  • Ceilings, walls and skirting for damp, seepage and cracks
  • All taps and drains run, traps refilled, flat aired
  • Pest signs — droppings, nesting, termite trails
  • Electrical and water meter readings
  • Doors, locks, grilles and balcony condition
  • Society notices, circulars and dues status

Plus a further ten checks on every visit, listed in full in your report.

WHAT YOU GET

Room-by-room photographs, meter readings recorded each visit, and a written note on anything that has changed since last time. We do not collect or hold your post and couriers.

AND THEN WE DO THE WORK

Damp and seepage repair. Plumbing and electrical fixes. Pest control. Deep cleaning before you arrive or before a tenant moves in. Painting and minor civil work. Paying society dues on your instruction and sending you the receipt. Meeting a technician so you do not have to find someone who will.

Each job is quoted separately before anything starts, and nothing happens without your written approval. You get the vendor invoice and before-and-after photographs.

Rented flat

▼
WHAT GOES WRONG WHEN NOBODY VISITS

Rent arrives late, then later, then with explanations. The flat is sublet without your knowledge. Damage accumulates and is only discovered at handover, by which time the deposit argument is already lost. Society compliance lapses in your name.

WHAT WE CHECK
  • Rent received, against the agreed date
  • Tenant in occupancy — the named tenant, not someone else
  • Flat condition against the move-in record
  • Maintenance issues reported by the tenant
  • Society dues and compliance
  • Agreement and renewal dates tracked
AND THEN WE DO THE WORK

Following up when rent is late. Attending to repairs the tenant reports, quoted and approved by you first. Move-in and move-out condition reports with photographs, so the deposit is settled on evidence rather than argument. Coordinating handover and preparing the flat between tenants.

Each job is quoted separately before anything starts, and nothing happens without your written approval.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not collect your rent. Rent is paid by your tenant directly into your account. We verify that it arrived, and we follow up when it does not.

Inherited or ancestral property

▼
WHAT GOES WRONG

Often the owner does not know precisely what they own. Boundaries are described by landmarks that no longer exist. A relative or long-standing occupant is in possession. Records are incomplete. Nothing is urgent until it suddenly is.

WHAT WE DO

A one-time survey visit. We locate the property, photograph it, record its present condition and who is in occupation, and give you a written account of what is actually there. What you do next is your decision — and if it needs a lawyer, we will say so plainly.

AND THEN WE DO THE WORK

Most inherited property needs something before it needs a decision. Clearing years of accumulated waste and overgrowth. Re-establishing a boundary that has become unclear on the ground. Securing the property with fencing and signage. Then regular visits, so it does not drift again while you work out what to do with it.

Each job is quoted separately, approved by you before it starts.

Under construction

▼
WHAT GOES WRONG

Progress updates come from the party with an interest in the progress sounding good. Delays are described as minor until possession is a year late.

WHAT WE DO

Scheduled visits to your unit and the project, photographed and dated. Stage of work recorded. Snagging observations at handover stage. You get an account that is not the builder's account.

AND THEN WE DO THE WORK

Following up with the builder's site office on your behalf on points raised in the report. Attending handover and recording the snag list with photographs. Once you have possession, taking over regular care of the flat — locked or tenanted.

Each job is quoted separately, approved by you before it starts.

The report is the product.

2. Fixed Camera Positions

We photograph from the same fixed positions every visit. Same corner, same angle, same frame. One report is a snapshot. Four reports are a record — you can see the boundary, the fence and the dumping holding steady or changing, quarter by quarter.

Every report names the person who visited. If you have a question about what is in it, there is someone to ask.

We do not take Power of Attorney.

We do not ask for Power of Attorney, and we do not hold your original title documents. We have never taken either, and we do not intend to.

Everything we do — visiting, photographing, clearing, checking a boundary, meeting a plumber — is done under a service agreement and your written instruction. None of it requires us to stand in your place legally.

If a specific act ever needs formal authorisation, we will tell you what it is, and you should have your own lawyer draft a limited authorisation covering that act alone. We will not draft it, and we will not suggest anything broader.

We are a property care service. We are not your legal representative.

What we do not do

  • We do not take Power of Attorney or hold original documents
  • We do not collect your rent
  • We do not give legal advice, or opinions on title
  • We do not act as brokers, and we do not sell or lease your property
  • We do not carry out any work, or spend any money, without your written approval
  • We do not claim any government affiliation, approval or empanelment

Who you are dealing with

PropVigil started because my father kept being asked.

V P Srinivasa has worked in real estate in Bengaluru since the early 2000s. In that time, people close to him — friends, relatives, former clients who had moved abroad — would ask him to go and look at a site. Check whether anything had been dumped. See if the fence was still standing. Send a photograph.

He did it because they asked, and because there was nobody else they could ask.

Over the years it became clear how many owners are in that position and do not have a Srinivasa to call. So we made it a service, with a schedule, a report and a price, instead of a favour.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know you actually visited?
▼

Every photograph in your report carries GPS coordinates and a timestamp. The metadata is intact and we will send you the original files if you ask.

How do I know the photographs are not reused from an earlier visit?
▼

We shoot from fixed positions each time, so consecutive reports show the same scene with the light, the season and the ground conditions different. Reused images are obvious in that format. The metadata settles it either way.

Do you need Power of Attorney?
▼

No. We do not take it and we do not hold original documents.

Do you handle my money?
▼

Only for work you have approved. Rent is paid to you directly by your tenant. We verify and follow up; we do not collect.

How are repairs and clearing paid for?
▼

We quote before any work. You approve in writing. You receive the vendor invoice and photographs of the completed work. Our charge is stated separately, so you always know what is the vendor's cost and what is ours.

What if you find a problem?
▼

We tell you the same day, with photographs, rather than waiting for the scheduled report. Then we tell you what your options are, what each would cost, and whether it needs a lawyer rather than us.
